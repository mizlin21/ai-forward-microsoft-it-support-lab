# AI Operational Metrics

## Purpose

This document contains simulated operational metrics for AI-assisted workflows in a Microsoft-based enterprise IT environment.

The goal is to demonstrate how organizations may evaluate the operational impact of AI-assisted support processes while maintaining governance, auditability, and human oversight.

These metrics are simulated for portfolio demonstration purposes.

## Overview

The following operational areas were evaluated:

- ticket triage
- onboarding workflows
- offboarding workflows
- escalation handling
- troubleshooting documentation
- operational reporting
- workflow standardization

## Simulated Operational Metrics

| Operational Area | Traditional Workflow | AI-Assisted Workflow | Estimated Improvement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ticket summarization | Manual | AI-assisted | ~80% faster |
| Ticket categorization | Manual review | AI-assisted recommendation | Improved consistency |
| Troubleshooting note creation | Manual drafting | AI-assisted drafting | ~60% faster |
| Escalation summary creation | Manual formatting | AI-assisted summary generation | ~75% faster |
| Onboarding checklist preparation | Manual creation | AI-generated template | ~70% faster |
| Offboarding documentation | Manual updates | AI-assisted summaries | ~65% faster |
| Reporting workflow preparation | Manual formatting | AI-assisted formatting | ~50% faster |

## AI Governance Alignment

This environment follows the principle:

```text
AI assists → humans review → humans approve → actions documented
```

AI-generated outputs should remain reviewable and auditable.

## Example Workflow Improvements

### Traditional Support Workflow

```text
Technician manually reviews ticket
      ↓
Technician categorizes issue
      ↓
Technician drafts notes
      ↓
Technician escalates manually
      ↓
Documentation updated manually
```

### AI-Assisted Support Workflow

```text
AI summarizes ticket
      ↓
AI suggests category and severity
      ↓
Technician reviews recommendations
      ↓
AI drafts operational notes
      ↓
Technician approves actions
      ↓
Workflow documented
```

## Example AI Risk Controls

| Risk | Governance Control |
|---|---|
| Unsafe AI-generated script | Human review required |
| Incorrect escalation recommendation | Technician validation |
| Hallucinated troubleshooting guidance | Technical verification |
| Sensitive data exposure | Prompt restrictions |
| Unauthorized operational action | Approval workflows |
