# Employee AI Prompt Guide

## Purpose

This document provides example AI prompt guidance for employees in a Microsoft-based enterprise environment.

The goal is to demonstrate how organizations can help employees use AI tools safely, consistently, and productively while maintaining governance and security controls.

This guide focuses on operational productivity use cases rather than unrestricted AI usage.

## AI Usage Principles

Employees should follow these principles when using AI tools:

- AI assists employees, not replaces decision-making.
- Sensitive company information should not be pasted into AI tools.
- AI-generated outputs should be reviewed before use.
- Employees remain responsible for final content and decisions.
- AI should be used to improve productivity and operational consistency.

## Approved AI Use Cases

Examples of approved AI-assisted tasks include:

- summarizing meeting notes
- drafting emails
- organizing project tasks
- creating document outlines
- rewriting internal communications
- generating brainstorming ideas
- summarizing operational procedures
- improving documentation formatting

## Restricted AI Use Cases

Examples of restricted or prohibited AI usage include:

- sharing passwords or secrets
- sharing confidential employee data
- uploading sensitive customer information
- bypassing security controls
- generating unauthorized administrative actions
- submitting unreviewed AI-generated work as final output
- requesting harmful or unsafe operational actions

## AI Review Expectations

Employees should verify:

- factual accuracy
- operational correctness
- policy alignment
- formatting quality
- confidential data exposure risks
- business appropriateness

before using AI-generated content.

## Security and Governance Notes

- Employees should not paste confidential data into AI tools.
- AI-generated outputs should remain reviewable.
- Sensitive operational information should remain protected.
- AI usage should follow company policies and security requirements.
- Employees remain accountable for final outputs.

## Human Oversight Principles

This guide follows these principles:

- AI assists, humans approve.
- Employees remain responsible for decisions.
- AI-generated outputs require review.
- Sensitive information requires protection.
- Governance and auditability remain important.
