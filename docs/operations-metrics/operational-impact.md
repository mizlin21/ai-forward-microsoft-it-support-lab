# Operational Impact Assessment

## Purpose

This document outlines simulated operational improvements associated with AI-assisted workflows in a Microsoft-based enterprise IT environment.

The goal is to demonstrate how AI operationalization can improve support efficiency, workflow consistency, documentation quality, and operational scalability while maintaining governance and human oversight.

## Overview

This simulated environment explores how AI-assisted operational workflows may support:

- IT support operations
- onboarding and offboarding
- ticket triage
- endpoint governance
- operational documentation
- escalation workflows
- employee enablement

AI is used to assist operational workflows rather than replace human decision-making.

## Operational Improvement Areas

The following areas were evaluated:

- ticket triage workflows
- documentation creation
- onboarding processes
- operational consistency
- troubleshooting workflows
- escalation documentation
- reporting workflows

## Governance Considerations

Operational improvements should not bypass governance controls.

This environment maintains:

- human oversight
- approval workflows
- auditability
- security review requirements
- documentation standards
- operational accountability

## AI Governance Alignment

This simulated environment follows the principle:

```text
AI assists → humans review → humans approve → actions documented
```

AI-generated outputs should remain reviewable and auditable.

## Example Operational Risk Controls

| Risk | Governance Control |
|---|---|
| Unsafe AI-generated guidance | Human review required |
| Incorrect ticket categorization | Technician validation |
| Sensitive data exposure | Restrict sensitive prompts |
| Unsafe PowerShell recommendations | Security review before execution |
| Overreliance on AI outputs | Human accountability maintained |

## Example Workflow Comparison

### Traditional Workflow

```text
Employee submits ticket
      ↓
Technician manually reviews ticket
      ↓
Technician categorizes issue
      ↓
Technician drafts documentation
      ↓
Technician escalates if needed
      ↓
Resolution documented manually
```

### AI-Assisted Workflow

```text
Employee submits ticket
      ↓
AI summarizes and categorizes issue
      ↓
Technician reviews recommendations
      ↓
AI drafts documentation
      ↓
Technician approves actions
      ↓
Resolution documented and reviewed
```
